About

I am a Researcher at the Shanghai Academy of AI for Science (SAIS) , working with Prof. Siyu Zhu . My current research focuses on AI agents. I am interested in moving beyond content generation toward systems that can carry out useful tasks in collaboration with people. My background spans generative modeling, efficient inference, and computational imaging.

Long-term vision

My long-term ambition is to contribute to artificial general intelligence that expands human capabilities. I hope to help build AI that reduces repetitive labor, broadens access to knowledge and tools, and enables new forms of collaboration and social organization. I am motivated not only by what AI can automate, but also by how its benefits can be broadly shared—giving people greater freedom to pursue creative, meaningful, and self-directed work.

I received my Ph.D. in Optical Engineering from Zhejiang University in June 2025, where I was affiliated with the State Key Laboratory of Modern Optical Instrumentation . My doctoral research focused on computational photography and image restoration. I also participated in the Pujiang National Laboratory Joint Training Ph.D. Program , with joint supervision from Prof. Yueting Chen and Prof. Tianfan Xue .

Before joining SAIS, I worked as a Senior Vision Algorithm Engineer at HONOR on diffusion-based mobile imaging. My experience in imaging research and industry informs my interest in building AI systems that work under real-world quality and efficiency constraints.

Research Directions

Current focus

AI Agents & Human–AI Collaboration

I am working on AI agents, with the goal of turning model capabilities into useful assistance with real-world tasks. I am interested in how these systems can complement human expertise and expand what people can accomplish.

Supporting research

Generative Modeling & Efficient Inference

My work involves language, image, and video generation, model evaluation, and efficient inference. I am interested in the trade-offs among output quality, reliability, and computational cost, particularly in practical generative systems.

Research foundation

Computational Imaging & Restoration

My earlier research combines imaging models with learning-based reconstruction to address motion blur, low light, platform jitter, and atmospheric turbulence. Applications include mobile photography and high-resolution remote sensing.

The publications below reflect my work in computational imaging and restoration; AI agents are my current research focus.
