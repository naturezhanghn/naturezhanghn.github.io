About Me

I am a Researcher at the Shanghai Academy of AI for Science (SAIS), working with Prof. Siyu Zhu. My current research focuses on multimodal foundation models, especially vision-language models (VLMs), multimodal understanding, generation, and editing.

I am interested in building multimodal AI systems that can understand and generate images, videos, language, and sensor-derived visual signals. My recent interests include vision-language alignment, multimodal reasoning, image/video generation, visual editing, and efficient deployment of multimodal models.

My previous work was rooted in computational imaging, event-based vision, low-light and HDR imaging, remote sensing restoration, and physics-guided reconstruction. This background provides a unique perspective for studying multimodal models in real-world visual perception, generation, editing, and AI-assisted scientific imaging.

Joint Training Ph.D. Experience: During my Ph.D., I was a member of the inaugural cohort of the Pujiang National Laboratory Joint Training Ph.D. Program and conducted joint research at Shanghai AI Lab from 2023 to 2025. This experience shaped my research interests in foundation models, generative AI, computer vision, and multimodal intelligence.

I earned my Ph.D. from Zhejiang University in June 2025. Prior to SAIS, I was a Senior Vision Algorithm Engineer at Honor, where I worked on AI-native imaging pipelines and real-world camera systems. My doctoral research was jointly advised by Dr. Yueting Chen and Prof. Tianfan Xue.

I obtained my B.S. from Central South University, where I received the National Scholarship and was recognized as an Outstanding Graduate of Hunan Province. My Master's research at Zhejiang University was advised by Prof. Huajun Feng.
