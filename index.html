I work on multimodal foundation models, with a current focus on vision-language models, multimodal understanding, generation, and visual editing. My research is motivated by a simple question: how can large multimodal models understand, manipulate, and generate visual content in ways that are useful for real-world perception, creative editing, and scientific imaging?

About

I am a Researcher at the Shanghai Academy of AI for Science, working with Prof. Siyu Zhu. My current research centers on multimodal foundation models, especially vision-language models, multimodal reasoning, image/video generation, and visual editing.

Before joining SAIS, I was a Senior Vision Algorithm Engineer at Honor, where I worked on AI-native imaging pipelines and real-world camera systems. My earlier research background spans computational imaging, event-based vision, low-light and HDR imaging, image restoration, and physics-guided reconstruction.

I received my Ph.D. from Zhejiang University in 2025. During my doctoral study, I was jointly trained at Shanghai AI Lab through the Pujiang National Laboratory Joint Training Ph.D. Program, advised by Dr. Yueting Chen and Prof. Tianfan Xue.

Research direction: I am particularly interested in connecting VLMs and generative models with visual editing, real-world imaging systems, and sensor-derived visual signals. AI for scientific imaging is one of the application areas I care about.
